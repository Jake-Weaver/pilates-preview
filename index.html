Questions

Good to know

I've never done Pilates. Is that okay?

Yes. Most of our clients are. Your first session is private.

I have an injury or chronic pain. Can I still come?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we'll plan around it. If you're under a doctor's care, bring any guidance you've been given.

Do I need the reformer, or is mat enough?

Both work. The reformer is best for rebuilding strength. Mat is the budget-friendly way to keep it up.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and grip socks. We have socks if you forget.

Where are you located?

Schuylkill County, close to Pottsville, Schuylkill Haven and Orwigsburg. You'll get the address when you book.

What's your cancellation policy?

24 hours' notice for private sessions, please.
