About us

Local. Small.
Hands-on.

Riverstone started with one reformer and a few neighbors whose backs were sore. They told their friends. It grew from there.

Who we are

From here, for here

We live in Schuylkill County. Folks here work on their feet, lift for a living and can't afford to be laid up. That's who we built this for.

No fancy gym. Just good instruction, small classes and people who know your name.
